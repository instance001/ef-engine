# Glossary

## Entropy

Unresolved search disorder:

- too many possible moves
- too much ambiguity
- too many repeated bad routes

In this repo, `entropy` is a search-routing term. It is not a thermodynamic
claim.

## Folding

The act of converting unresolved disorder into usable structure for future
routing.

## Attempt

A bounded move toward an outcome.

## Stuck

A truthful state where the current route has ceased making valid progress.

## Vault

A preserved failure record containing evidence from one or more failed attempts.

## Retry Delta

The explicit record of what changed between one attempt and the previous one.

## Triangulation

The comparison of bounded attempts to isolate a shared lock point.

## Lock Point

The exact structural blocker behind one or more visible failures.

## Metabolization

The conversion of a triangulated failure into durable negative knowledge.

This is an engineering metaphor for promoting an evidenced failure pattern into
a reusable constraint. It is not a biological or cognitive claim.

## Constraint

A reusable boundary that blocks known bad future routes under defined
conditions.

## Eureka Cascade

The accelerated effect where one metabolized lesson blocks multiple related
future failures.

The term describes a routing effect, not sudden magical insight or guaranteed
global improvement.

## Routing Consequence

The practical future effect of a metabolized constraint on what the engine
should or should not try next.
