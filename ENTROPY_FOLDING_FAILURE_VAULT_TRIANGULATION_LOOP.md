# Entropy Folding Engine: Failure Vault and Triangulation Loop

## Purpose

This document captures the core mechanism the Entropy Folding Engine (`EF
Engine`) is being built around while the architectural context is still fresh.

`Entropy Folding` is not only a name.
It is the theory of motion underneath the engine.

The engine encounters disorder, ambiguity, failed motion, and branching
possibility space.
It then folds that entropy into a more structured form:

- raw failure becomes evidence
- scattered evidence becomes triangulation
- triangulation becomes constraint
- constraint becomes cleaner routing

The engine improves by folding chaotic failed search into usable structure.

The central idea is simple:

1. attempt forward motion
2. detect when motion has genuinely failed or become stuck
3. preserve the failure as evidence instead of smoothing it over
4. retry differently rather than repeating the same move
5. isolate the exact failure pattern through triangulation
6. convert that pattern into a durable constraint
7. improve the odds of future success by routing around known bad moves

This is not a system that claims perfect success on first pass.
It is a system that becomes more reliable by metabolizing failure honestly and
folding failed search into navigational order.

## Core Loop

The Entropy Folding Engine loop can be stated in plain language:

- hit failure
- vault it
- try again differently
- triangulate the lock
- metabolize the lesson
- move forward with better boundaries

Or more formally:

1. `Attempt`
The engine executes a bounded move toward a requested outcome.

2. `Stuck Detection`
The engine determines that the move failed, stalled, or cannot proceed honestly
under current conditions.

3. `Vaulting`
The failure is recorded as a first-class artifact with evidence, not treated as
an inconvenience to be hidden.

4. `Fractal Rerouting`
The next attempt must differ in a meaningful way: smaller decomposition,
different ordering, different method, different tool posture, tighter scope, or
other evidence-justified variation.

5. `Triangulation`
Multiple bounded attempts are compared to identify the exact failure point, not
just the visible symptom.

6. `Metabolization`
Once the failure pattern is understood, it is converted into a reusable
constraint, warning, or routing rule that prevents future waste.

7. `Forward Motion`
Future attempts gain a higher chance of success because the bad path has been
named, evidenced, and fenced off.

## Why It Is Entropy Folding

In this engine, `entropy` refers to unresolved search:

- too many possible moves
- too many unclear explanations
- too many repeated bad routes
- too much disorder between intention and successful execution

`Folding` is the act of compressing that disorder into structure the engine can
actually use.

The fold happens when:

- a vague failure becomes a specific failure
- a specific failure becomes an exact lock point
- an exact lock point becomes a reusable boundary
- a reusable boundary reduces future uncertainty

So the engine does not merely remember.
It reduces disorder by reshaping failure into constraint-bearing structure.

## Why This Matters

Most systems lose value when they fail because they discard the failure.
Entropy Folding Engine is intended to do the opposite.

Failure is not merely a bad outcome.
Failure is raw material.

In entropy-folding terms, failure is high-entropy material waiting to be
compressed into useful order.

If a failure is:

- preserved clearly
- classified honestly
- retried with variation
- narrowed to its exact cause
- converted into a reusable guardrail

then the system has gained capability even when the immediate attempt did not
succeed.

That gain is the engine.

That conversion is the fold.

## Key Principles

### 1. Honest Failure Over Pretend Success

The engine must never blur a hard block into vague optimism.
If it is stuck, it should say so and preserve why.

### 2. Evidence Before Theory

Constraints should emerge from observed repeated failure, not from abstract
preference or premature doctrine.

### 3. Retry Must Mean Change

A retry is only meaningful if the next move differs in a bounded and inspectable
way.
Repeated identical failure is not search.

### 4. Triangulation Over Guessing

The purpose of repeated attempts is not brute force.
It is comparative isolation of the exact lock point.

### 5. Constraints Are Metabolized Memory

The constraints library is the engine's accumulated negative memory:

- what not to do
- under which conditions not to do it
- what kind of replacement posture is safer

### 6. Forward Motion Is Probabilistic Improvement

The goal is not magical certainty.
The goal is increasing the odds of valid future progress by removing previously
observed failure paths.

### 7. Folding Must Reduce Search Disorder

If a lesson does not reduce future confusion, ambiguity, or wasted branching,
then the fold is weak.

Good entropy folding should:

- reduce repeated error
- reduce wasted route exploration
- reduce ambiguity about what failed
- increase clarity about what should happen next

## System Vocabulary

### Failure

A bounded attempt that cannot complete honestly, cannot verify, or cannot
continue without violating constraints or reality.

### Stuck

A practical state where the engine has ceased making truthful progress under the
current route.

### Vault

A preserved failure record containing the attempt, the context, the observed
evidence, and the relevant blocking signal.

### Fractal Reroute

A retry that changes the path at the appropriate scale rather than merely
repeating the same action again.

### Triangulation

The process of comparing bounded failed or divergent attempts to isolate the
exact failure mechanism.

### Metabolization

The act of converting a triangulated failure pattern into durable operational
memory such as a constraint, route block, caution rule, or retry policy.

In entropy-folding terms, metabolization is where observed disorder becomes
stable reusable structure.

### Constraint Library

A growing negative-memory system that fences off known bad moves and helps steer
future attempts toward safer routes.

## Operational Shape

At a high level, Entropy Folding Engine should eventually preserve at least
these artifacts:

- attempt records
- stuck or failure records
- vault entries
- retry-delta records describing what changed
- triangulation sessions
- exact failure-point findings
- constraint proposals
- approved constraints
- forward-routing consequences of approved constraints

This keeps learning inspectable.
The system should be able to show not only that it improved, but how.

## Non-Goals

EF Engine is not intended to:

- claim universal first-pass success
- hide failure to protect appearances
- treat all retries as equivalent
- build doctrine from intuition alone
- confuse symptoms with root locks
- accumulate constraints without evidence

## Working Thesis

Entropy Folding Engine increases its usefulness by turning failure into routing
intelligence.

The sequence is:

- failure becomes evidence
- evidence becomes triangulation
- triangulation becomes constraint
- constraint becomes avoidance
- avoidance creates cleaner future motion

This is called Entropy Folding because each pass reduces chaotic search and
reforms it into more ordered future behavior.

That is the core engine.

## Initial One-Line Summary

Entropy Folding Engine is a failure-metabolizing navigation system: when it
gets stuck, it vaults the failure, reroutes differently, triangulates the exact
lock, folds that disorder into a constraint, and uses the new boundary to
improve future success.
